③ BUS TRANSMISSION — Word sent on ARINC 429 wire
The 32-bit word is sent LSB-first at 100 kbps. Each bit takes 10 microseconds on the wire. Total transmission time = 320 µs.
Bit 1 (LSB) → ← Bit 32 (Parity)
Transmission speed: 100,000 bits/sec (High Speed)  |  Word period: 320 µs
④ RECEIVER DECODES — Extracting values from 32 bits
The cockpit display unit (ND/MFD) receives the raw 32-bit word, reads the label to identify data type, checks SSM for validity, verifies parity, then extracts each field.
